## Scrum
Scrum is an agile method that provides a framework for agile project organization and planning.
It focus on delivering products in iterative cycles called sprints, typically lasting 2-4 weeks
### Scrum Terminology

- Product Owner

- Scrum

- Velocity

- Product Backlog

- Self-organizing teams

# Product Backlog
The product backlog is a list of what needs to be done to complete the development of the product
The itmes on this list are called product backlog items (PBIs)
The product backlog may inclnude a variety of different items such as development activities and desirable engineering improvments.
The product backlog should always be prioritized so that  the items that be implemented first are at the top of the list

## PBL States
- Ready for consideration
- Ready for refinement
- Ready for implementation
## PBL Activities
- Refinement
- Estimation
- Creation
- Prioritization
