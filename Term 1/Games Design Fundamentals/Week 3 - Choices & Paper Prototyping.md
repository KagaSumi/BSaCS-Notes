## Decision Scope
- Once the player makes a choice, what is the scope of its impact?
	- What are the consequences (beneficial or harmful)
	- How much of the game does it affect?
	- For how long does this affect?
	- How much does the choice constrain other decisions the player might make?
## Choice
- What happened before the player was given a choice?
	- What is current game state? What just happened?
	- Broken too much hidden information (eg fog of war)
- How is the possibility of choice conveyed to the player?
	- What feedback is the am giving?
	- Broken not knowing what to do next
- How did the player make the choice?
	- what physical mechanisms did the player use to input the choice? (eg: play card, push button)
	- Broken not being able to give input
- What was the result of the choice?
	- how does it affect the system?
	- BROKEN feeling like your decisions are arbitrary
	- BROKEN not knowing if your action had an outcome
- How is the result of the choice conveyed to the player?
	- Visual, audio, haptic, output
	- BROKEEN losing a game without knowing why
## Player Choices
>"A game is a series of interesting choices" - Sid Meier, Civilization

A "hollow" choice is one with no consequences
	Choice, but no effect on outcome

A "obvious" choice isn't really a choice at all
	Choice, but clearly one is better

A "uninformed" choice doesn't give you enough information
	Choice, but goal is not clear
### Interesting Choices
- Give you enough information that you can make an informed decision
- Have some uncertainty (the choice isn't obvious)
- Have Consequences
- Those Consequences are easy to assess, but the choice still feels difficult
