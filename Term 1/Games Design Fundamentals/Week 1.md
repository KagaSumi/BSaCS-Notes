What is a game?

- replay ability
- win/lose condition

- rules/procedures
- goals/challenges
- numbers. resources.
- player choices
- strategy
- randomness
- learning curve/ skill
- difficulty curves
- feedback
- boundaries

## Design Document
2-3 Pages
- Name
- Title
- Tagline
	1 Sentence that sums your game
- Essence Statement
	1 Paragraph Elevator Pitch, "Ill be reading this outloud"
- Players + Camera + Genre
	Solo or multiplayer? First person or third person?
	 What Genre
-  Intended Platforms,
	- PC
- Game Overview: Core Mechanic + Core Loop
	- Give a high-level description of the gameplay, what is the goal of the game? What gameplay will the player be engaged in to attempt the goal?
- Stretch Goals
	- ideas on how to expand the game if there is time
- 3-5 Key Features
		Bullet points of the gameplay you described in the game overview - what will "sell" the game to players? What will make the game fun to play?
- Design Pillars
	2-3 Goals you have for your design- these are the criteria against which all of your designing decisions will be measured. Get specific here.
