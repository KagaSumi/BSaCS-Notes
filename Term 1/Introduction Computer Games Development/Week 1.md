## Game development Process
We will mimic the **game development process** during this course
1. Establish the vision and create a design document
2. Break the document down into features
3. Establish milestones and scope features within time and budget constraints
4. Establish feature delivery schedule
5. Meet weekly to track progression on the features
6. Update issues daily
