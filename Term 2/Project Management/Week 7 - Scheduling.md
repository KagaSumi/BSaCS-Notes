# Scheduling

>How to know if you have successful project
Iron triangle met:
> Time, Cost, Scope

## Task Identification
- Your **WBS** is your list of tasks and activates.
- Estimates of each tasks/activity provide you with a duration
### Next Steps
- Determine which task/activates are Level of Effort ad over what period
- For each tasks identify the dependencies
- Layout the tasks over time, considering the dependencies
- Identify the **critical path** (longest sequence of tasks)
- Decide if you can apply schedule compression
- Assign resources to the tasks

## Critical Path
Longest and the Shortest line of tasks

Forward Pass:
- early Start/Finish
- Duration, Lab
## Schedule Compression
PMI says:
- Fast Tracking (Do something ahead of schedule)
- Crashing (Add more resources)
	- Does increase costs
- At some point can't compress any further

# Agile Scheduling
## Overly Optimistic Schedules
Watch for the following when planning your schedule:
- External immovable deadlines (tradeshow, Christmas)
- Under estimating (trying to make the deal)
- Upper management choosing "best case scenario" rather than a arrange
- Manager ineffective during customer negotiations
- Project Manager believes team will work harder with tighter deadlines
- Developers under estimate (too confident of skills)
