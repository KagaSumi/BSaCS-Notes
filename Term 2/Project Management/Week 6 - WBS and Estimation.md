# Introduction and Review
## Risk Management
- Risk Management is a key part of early and ongoing Project management
- Risk Mitigation are often incorporated into and change Project Scope
## Requirements
- Project Scope includes Product Scope and Product Scope includes Requirements
- Requirements change because the Customer and the Project Team are constantly learning
## Task Estimation
- Requirements must be translated into Tasks.
- Then, the duration of the work/effort required to complete each Task must be estimated.
- Estimates are uncertain because they are predictions and predicting the future is hard.
# Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
> the process of subdividing project deliverables and project work into smaller, more manageable components.

WBS is a hierarchical **decomposition** of total scope of work to be carried out by the project team. 

The goal of WBS is to distill down the actions to create deliverable.

The lowest level of components of in the WBS are called **Work Packages** or **Tasks**

A work package can be used to group the activities where work is scheduled and estimated, monitored.
## Agile
Using Agile values and principles doesn't mean you don't need a WBS

### How to Incorporate
- Typically Sprints are timeboxed, (Sprint duration)
- Scrum team can determine tasks and overall effort then refine these estimates during planning for each Sprint.
- During Sprint Planning, the Scrum team should commit to only the work it can complete (increment) within that time box.
- In the Sprint Retrospective, the Scrum team can determine and refine its maximum capacity for each Sprint (Velocity) and incorporate that into next Sprint Planning

## WBS
The WBS forms the **Scope Baseline**.
- It identifies all the work to be done for the project

Helps to form the basis for estimates, schedule and monitoring and controls

Make sure you identify all the tasks required to execute the project\*

For large project, you can use progressive elaboration

# Control Scope
Monitor the status of the Project and Project Scope manage/control changes to Scope Baseline

Enable maintenance of the Scope baseline throughout the project includes
- **Handling change request** to the Product and Project Scope
- **Handling Issues** -> corrective actions
- **Handling risks** -> mitigations

Change is inevitable, so Change Control process is Mandatory for every project

**Uncontrolled** product or project scope expansion called **Scope Creep** (or feature creep or product scope expansion)
