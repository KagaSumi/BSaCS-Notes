# Project Management Introduction
## Assignment 1 - Project Charter
An overview of the project describing at a high-level:
- What is the project/
- Why are we doing it ?
- Who will be doing it?
- When is it needed?
- Where will we be doing it?
Derived from inputs from customers, business development, salespeople, management, other stakeholders.
Typically, input is a Statement of Work (SOW) negotiated with the customer
Output is typically a document or presentation
## Assignment 2 - Project Plan
Project Management Plan -or- Project Execution Plan
Describes the details of how the project will be executed to meet the stakeholder requirements, based on the Charter as an input

Typically Covers:
- Project Lifecycle
- Scope Management
- Requirements Management 
	- i.e., Collection and Definition
- Schedule Management
- Cost management 
- Risk Management
- Stakeholder Identification

Output is usually several documents
