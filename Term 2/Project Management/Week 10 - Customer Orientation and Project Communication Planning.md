# Project Communication Planning

1. Developing a strategy 
## Communication Activates
**Internal** - Focus on stakeholders within project within org
**External** - Focus on external stake holders such as customer, vendors, other projects org. government the public and environmental advocates
**Formal** - Reports, formal meeting (both regular and ad hoc) meeting agendas and minutes ,stake holder briefings, and presentations
**Informal** - general communications activates using emails social media websites and informal ad hoc discussions
**Hierarchical Focus** - the position of the stakeholder or group with respect to project team will affect the format and content of the message, in the following ways
- **Upward** - Senior management Stakeholders
- **Downward** - The team and others who will contribute to the work of the project
- **Horizontal** - peers of the project manager or team.
- **Official** - annual reports; reports to regulator or government body
